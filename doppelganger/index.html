Defence

  • Register the closest variants yourself. Buy the homoglyph and common-TLD lookalikes nearest your name (the red and pulsing dots first) so an attacker can't.
  • Watch Certificate Transparency logs. Monitor crt.sh / CT feeds for new TLS certificates issued on lookalike names — a cert often appears days before a phishing site goes live.
  • Harden email and train staff. Enforce SPF, DKIM and a strict DMARC policy, add external-sender banners, and teach staff to spot lookalike senders (rn vs m, 0 vs o, swapped TLDs). Unicode/IDN homoglyphs go further than the ASCII set shown here.

No tracking and no registration. The only network calls are DNS-over-HTTPS queries to dns.google for the candidate names; your domain never leaves these DNS lookups.
